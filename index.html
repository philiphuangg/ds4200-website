Welcome to My Website

About Me

Hey! I'm Linxuan, a fourth-year student at Northeastern University studying Data Science. I made this website for my DS4200 Information Visualization class, but I also wanted to use it as a space to share a little about myself and some of the things I've been learning.

I've always been interested in working with numbers and figuring out what they actually mean. What I like about data visualization is that it takes something that might look confusing at first and turns it into something much easier to understand. I think it's pretty cool how a simple chart can sometimes explain something better than a whole page of numbers.

Throughout college, I've worked on different projects involving data analysis, coding, and statistics. Some have definitely been more challenging than others, but they've helped me figure out what I enjoy and what I want to keep improving on. Recently, I've been learning more about creating interactive visualizations and how small design choices, like colors, labels, and layouts, can make a big difference in how people interpret information.

Outside the Classroom

When I'm not studying or working on assignments, I like to stay active and spend time doing things I enjoy. I'm a big fan of tennis and soccer, whether that's playing with friends or watching a good match. I also really enjoy cooking and trying out new recipes whenever I have the time.

I think having hobbies outside of school is important, especially when most of my day involves staring at a computer screen. Whether I'm playing sports, cooking dinner, or just hanging out with friends, I enjoy finding ways to take a break and have some fun.
